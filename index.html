Operations & Safety Standard

How BOC Manages Every Refinery Dispatch

  • 1. Pre-Loading Document Check: Verification of Vehicle RC, Fitness, Insurance, Pollution (PUC), Permits, and calibration status before gate entry.
  • 2. Canonical Weighbridge Recording: Both loading refinery weight and unloading plant weight are recorded in exact kilograms (kg) and converted to metric tonnes (1,000 kg = 1.000 MT).
  • 3. Honest Status & Location Updates: Manual dispatch milestones are clearly marked as Manual; live GPS is displayed only when a verified hardware VLT device is active.
  • 4. Reconciled Trip Settlement: En-route HSD, Cash advances, Plant HSD, Plant Cash, and custom contract columns (S/L, S/E Amt) are transparently reconciled before final invoicing.
Knowledge Centre & FAQ

Key Factors in Bulk Bitumen Freight Billing

Q1: How is billable weight decided between Refinery and Unloading Plant?

Each customer contract explicitly defines whether freight is billed on Loaded Weight (MT), Unloaded Weight (MT), or Net Weight with agreed tolerance.

Q2: How are Plant HSD and Cash Advances adjusted?

All diesel (HSD Litres & Amount) and cash provided at loading or unloading plants are logged trip-by-trip and deducted transparently in the A4 Freight Statement.
